Privacy

This website

This site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no cookies of its own. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and your browser asks GitHub for the latest version when the page opens, so GitHub receives the usual request details such as your IP address. Downloads also come from GitHub.

The Cerebra app

Cerebra runs on your computer and keeps your records in a local database there. What leaves your computer is what you connect it to: requests to the AI models you choose and the services you sign in to, such as Gmail or Etsy. Those services handle that data under their own terms.

Cerebra also contacts public services to do its work, such as GitHub to check for updates, public map data to find businesses, the websites of those businesses, and a public DNS service, which receives a business's domain name to check that it can receive email.
