What it does

Builds software with a real review

One model plans the work, another writes the code, and a model from a different company reviews it. Your project's tests run before anything counts as done.

Finds work for you

It finds freelance jobs and local US businesses that need a website, reads what they actually need, and drafts proposals and emails in your voice for you to check.

Makes things to sell

Amazon KDP notebooks and planners with covers and listings, Etsy printables, and print-ready designs for shirts and mugs.

Keeps the money straight

Quotes, invoices with payment links, and orders and sales from Etsy, Shopify and Printify, all in one place.

Uses the right model for each job

Claude and ChatGPT through the subscriptions you already have, plus free open models and local models. If one runs out, the other can carry on.

Shows you what happened

A live view of every part at work and a plain-English report for every task: what was asked, who did what, and whether the tests passed.

You stay in charge

  1. Everything starts off. A new install has automation and sharing switched off until you choose.
  2. Your data stays with you. Your records live in a database on your computer, with a checked backup every day.
  3. You connect what you want. Sign in with your own accounts. Cerebra never sees your AI passwords.
  4. Updates come to you. Cerebra tells you when a new version is ready, and installs it when you say so.

Questions

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Yes, for now. Cerebra's installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. You can check your download against the SHA-256 shown above.

Do I need a Claude or ChatGPT account?

To use them, yes: Cerebra works through your own subscriptions. Free open models and local models are also supported.

Does it send emails or post things by itself?

Only if you turn automatic outreach on. In draft mode you read and send each first email yourself, and Cerebra then sends the follow-ups to businesses you've already emailed, stopping as soon as they reply. Automatic outreach has daily limits, and it pauses by itself if too many emails bounce.

Is it available for Mac or Linux?

The installer is for Windows.

How do I update?

Cerebra checks for new versions and offers them in Settings. You don't need a GitHub account.
