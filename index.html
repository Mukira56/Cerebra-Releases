What it does

Finds the right businesses

Pick the kinds of businesses you serve and the cities you work in. Cerebra lists them from public map data, finds their websites and their public email, and skips addresses that can't receive mail.

Reads each one first

It studies each business's own website: what they offer, what's missing, what they care about. Every email is built from what it found there, never from a template with a name swapped in.

Writes like you

Emails go out from your Gmail, in your voice, with your business details and an opt-out line, as US law requires. You can read and edit every email before it goes.

Follows up and stops on a reply

Polite follow-ups on the days you choose, spread through the day within a daily limit. When someone answers, or asks not to be contacted, Cerebra stops and shows you.

Protects your Gmail

Daily limits, a sending window, a check that every domain can receive mail, and an automatic pause if too many emails bounce.

Shows what it did

A results page counts emails sent, replies, bounces and opt-outs from your own records. Time saved is shown as an estimate, labelled as one.

You stay in charge

  1. Nothing goes out by itself until you say so. Start in draft mode and send each first email yourself; switch to automatic when you trust it.
  2. Your data stays with you. Prospects, emails and replies live in a database on your computer, with a checked backup every day.
  3. Your own accounts. Cerebra works through your Gmail and your Claude or ChatGPT subscription, or free models. It never sees your passwords.
  4. No lock-in. Your records stay readable and exportable whatever happens to your licence.

Questions

Who is it for?

Small US businesses that sell a service to other local businesses: web and marketing agencies, IT support, bookkeepers, designers, consultants. If a new client is worth a few hundred dollars or more to you, a handful of good replies pays for it.

Will it get me clients?

It does the finding, research, writing and follow-up, and it shows you exactly what happened. Whether people reply depends on your offer, your market and your emails, so we don't promise numbers. The free trial lets you see real replies before you pay.

Is cold email allowed?

Business email to other businesses is legal in the US when it follows the CAN-SPAM Act: honest subject and sender, your business address, and a working way to opt out, which you must honour. Cerebra adds the opt-out line, stops writing to anyone who opts out and keeps volumes low. You're responsible for your own campaigns; check the rules for your industry.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Yes, for now. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. You can check your download against the SHA-256 shown above.

Do I need a Claude or ChatGPT account?

A subscription you already have works best. Free open models are also supported for much of the writing.

Is it available for Mac?

Not yet: the app is for Windows 10 and 11.
